Although the Grand Hyatt Jin Mao, built in 1999, is showing its age—which lowered our expectations—it actually turned out to be a great stay. The doormen actively opened car doors and retrieved our luggage. The front desk accommodated our requests as much as possible, upgrading us to a Grand Club River View Room on the 80th floor facing the Puxi Bund. The room was well-maintained, feeling fresh and clean. In the Executive Lounge, the afternoon tea's blueberry scones and walnut cake were quite good (the coffee machine is located in the innermost corner), though the evening canapés were of mediocre quality—I had the salted salmon. Breakfast on the 56th floor was decent; if you walk to the northernmost buffet station and look inside, you'll find freshly pan-fried buns (shengjianbao) and potstickers, which were delicious. The room is 40 square meters, and the bathroom is small and compact. Still, it's totally worth visiting for the experience—both its exterior and interior are the most stunning among the 'three major skyscrapers'.

    The crowning glory of what was Shanghai's tallest building, until the arrival of the Shanghai World Financial Center (SWFC), the Grand Hyatt Shanghai (Shanghai Jinmao Junyue Dajiudian) occupies the 56th to 87th floors of the 88-story Jin Mao Tower. From the spectacular panorama of Pudong, guests can enjoy great views of the historic Bund with its Jazz Age architectural masterpieces, and easy access to a number of local Shanghai attractions such as the Yu Gardens and Century Park. The luxury shopping malls of Lujiazui are just minutes away.Guestrooms enjoy stunning views of the city's iconic oriental pearl TV Tower, the Bund and the surrounding space-age skyline, and include generously sized marble bathrooms with deep, indulgent bathtubs.Fine dining in the hotel's 8 restaurants and bars, includes delicious Italian, Japanese, Shanghainese, Cantonese cuisines and a prime grill, as well as a range of buffets.The 24-hour business center is fully equipped with secretarial services, while the hotel's concierge team is the most resourceful in the city. Board meetings, conferences and receptions can be held in the Grand Hyatt's function rooms, which can accommodate 12 to 1,200.Club Oasis offers a gym, therapies, treatments, whirlpool spas, hot and cold plunge pools, sauna and steam rooms, and relaxation lounges. The Sky Pool, with its two-story plate glass windows, offers spectacular 180-degree views over Shanghai.

    A First-Timer's Guide to the Area Around Grand Hyatt Shanghai

    Discover the pulsating heart of Shanghai as you step into the vibrant surroundings of Grand Hyatt Shanghai. Nestled in Pudong, this hotel not only offers luxurious accommodations but also serves as the perfect launchpad for exploring the dynamic city.

    Situated in the Lujiazui Financial District, Grand Hyatt Shanghai provides easy access to key attractions. The hotel stands amidst the glittering skyscrapers of Pudong, placing guests at the epicenter of modern Shanghai.

    Step outside and you're greeted by a tapestry of experiences. IFC Mall beckons with high-end shopping, while local markets offer a taste of authentic Shanghai. Culinary delights abound, from street food stalls to upscale dining.

    Within walking distance, iconic landmarks await. The Oriental Pearl Tower defines the skyline, the Bund offers historic charm, and the Power Station of Art showcases contemporary creativity. For tranquility, explore the classical beauty of Yu Garden.

    In the evening, head to the Sky Walk on the 100th floor of the Shanghai World Financial Center for panoramic views.

    Attractions Near Grand Hyatt Shanghai

    Oriental Pearl Tower (Landmark):

    A symbol of Shanghai, the Oriental Pearl Tower is an architectural marvel with its distinctive spheres. It houses observation decks offering panoramic views.

    Walking Distance: 1 mile from the hotel.

    Estimated Time: 5 minutes walking.

    Suitable for: Ideal for tourists interested in iconic landmarks and breathtaking cityscapes.

    The Bund (Historical Site):

    The Bund is a historic waterfront area along the Huangpu River, featuring colonial-era buildings and a picturesque promenade.

    Estimated Time: 10 minutes by car.

    Suitable for: History enthusiasts and those seeking a leisurely riverside stroll.

    IFC Mall (Shopping):

    IFC Mall is a premier shopping destination with luxury boutiques, international brands, and diverse dining options.

    Walking Distance: 1 mile from the hotel.

    Estimated Time: 5 minutes walking.

    Suitable for: Shopaholics and those looking for upscale retail therapy.

    Yu Garden (Nature and Culture)

    Yu Garden is a classical Chinese garden dating back to the Ming Dynasty, featuring pavilions, ponds, and intricate landscaping.

    Walking Distance: 3 miles from the hotel.

    Estimated Time: 10 minutes by car.

    Suitable for: Nature lovers, cultural explorers, and anyone seeking a tranquil escape.

    Power Station of Art (Art and Culture)

    Housed in a converted power plant, this contemporary art museum showcases a diverse range of Chinese and international artworks.

    Estimated Time: 15 minutes driving from the hotel.

    Suitable for: Art enthusiasts and those interested in modern cultural experiences.

    Sky Walk at Shanghai World Financial Center (Panoramic Views)

    Located on the 100th floor, the Sky Walk offers unparalleled panoramic views of Shanghai's skyline and beyond.

    Walking Distance: 0.5 miles from the hotel.

    Suitable for: Photography enthusiasts and anyone captivated by breathtaking cityscapes.

    Grand Hyatt Shanghai’s Ideal Retreats for Every Traveler

    Business Travelers:

    Grand Hyatt Shanghai is a haven for business travelers, offering proximity to the Lujiazui Financial District and convenient access to corporate offices. The hotel provides state-of-the-art meeting facilities, high-speed internet, and a dedicated business center, ensuring a seamless and productive stay for professionals on the go.

    Couples and Romance Seekers:

    The hotel's luxurious ambiance, coupled with breathtaking views of the cityscape, makes it an ideal retreat for couples seeking a romantic getaway. The intimate setting of the guest rooms, coupled with the romantic backdrop of Shanghai's skyline, creates an unforgettable experience for those looking to celebrate love.

    Culture Enthusiasts:

    For travelers eager to explore Shanghai's rich cultural heritage, Grand Hyatt Shanghai's proximity to iconic landmarks like the Bund, Yu Garden, and the Power Station of Art makes it an ideal base. Immerse yourself in the city's history and contemporary art scene, all of which are located within a short distance from the hotel.

    Shopping Aficionados:

    IFC Mall, one of Shanghai's premier shopping destinations, is located just minutes from the hotel. Fashion-forward guests can indulge in a retail therapy session, exploring international and luxury brands without venturing far from the hotel's doorstep.

    Food Connoisseurs:

    Grand Hyatt Shanghai itself is a culinary destination, offering a diverse range of dining experiences within the hotel premises. With authentic Chinese cuisine at Club Jin Mao to international delights at On56, food connoisseurs will find their taste buds satiated without leaving the hotel.

    Wellness and Leisure Seekers:

    The hotel's health club, spa, and indoor pool provide a rejuvenating escape for wellness-conscious travelers. After a day of exploration, guests can unwind with a spa treatment or enjoy a refreshing swim, ensuring a perfect balance of relaxation and adventure.

    Unique Facilities at Grand Hyatt Shanghai

    Business Center (Lobby Level 54):

    Grand Hyatt Shanghai's Business Center, strategically positioned on lobby level 54, offers a comprehensive array of office amenities. From top-notch equipment to confidential secretarial services, including translation, printing, and parcel wrapping, it caters to the diverse needs of business travelers. The center also provides private workspaces for rent on an hourly or daily basis, along with a conference room accommodating up to ten people.

    24-hour Fitness Center:

    For fitness enthusiasts, the 24-hour Fitness Centre at Grand Hyatt Shanghai is a haven of cutting-edge equipment. From cycling machines, spinners, treadmills, to cross trainers and strength machines, guests have access to a comprehensive range. Private training instructors are also available, ensuring a holistic wellness experience.

    Sky-High Pool:

    Indulge in the sky-high pool on level 57, offering breathtaking panoramas of the Shanghai skyline. The pool provides a refreshing retreat and a unique vantage point to unwind.

    Recreation on Level 57:

    Located on level 57, Grand Hyatt Shanghai's recreational facilities offer an oasis for business travelers to wind down. In addition to comprehensive spa treatments, guests can enjoy a swim in the sky pool, surrounded by the stunning Shanghai skyline.

    Diverse Dining Options:

    Grand Hyatt Shanghai boasts eight distinctive restaurants and bars, each catering to different culinary desires. From the all-day dining restaurant, On56, to the Japanese restaurant and lounge bar, Kobachi, and the renowned Club Jin Mao offering Shanghainese cuisine with panoramic views, the hotel ensures a gastronomic journey. Cloud 9 on the 87th floor and Pastry Boutique further contribute to the diverse dining experiences, providing ideal spaces for night gatherings and socializing over quality Nespresso coffees, handmade cakes, pastries, and chocolates.
